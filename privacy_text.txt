GREENIE PRIVACY NOTICE
Last updated: 28 October 2026

1. Who is responsible
Greenie is operated by Bean Co. For any question about your data,
write to greenie.app.team@gmail.com.

2. What data we store
- Account data: your email address and your password. The password is
  stored only as a salted hash. We cannot read it.
- Profile data: your nickname, first name, last name, date of birth
  and university.
- Activity data: the eco-actions you log (action, points, estimated
  CO2 saving, date and time) and the students you follow.

3. Why we store it
We use your data only to run Greenie:
- to create your account and log you in and out,
- to calculate your points, streak, CO2 estimate and badges,
- to show the weekly leaderboards,
- to let other students find your profile by nickname and follow you,
- to show you your own history,
- to send you a password-reset email if you ask for one.

4. What other students can see
Other students see only your nickname, university, total points,
points this week, streak and earned badges. Leaderboards show only
rank, nickname and points. Your email address, first name, last name
and date of birth are never shown to other users.

5. Who processes your data
- Database and login: Supabase, stored in an EU region (Ireland).
- Website files: GitHub Pages. As with any web host, your IP address
  may be processed when you open the site.
- Password-reset emails: sent through a Gmail account, so your email
  address is processed by Google for this purpose.
We do not use analytics, advertising or third-party trackers. We do
not sell your data or share it with anyone else. The people who
develop the app work with test accounts and seed data only and keep
no copy of real user data.

6. How long we keep your data
We keep your data until you delete your account. In the app, choose
"Delete my account". This removes your login, profile data, logged
actions and follow relations, and you disappear from all
leaderboards, search results and following lists. We keep no backups
of your data.

7. Your rights
You can ask us at greenie.app.team@gmail.com to give you a copy of
your data, correct it or delete it. We will answer as soon as we can.

8. Age
Greenie is for students who are at least 16 years old.

9. Security
Passwords are stored as salted hashes. All connections use HTTPS.
Access to the database is limited to the people who run the service.

10. Consent
By ticking the box on the registration form, you confirm that you
have read this notice and agree to the processing of your data as
described here. You can withdraw your consent at any time by deleting
your account.

11. Changes
If we change this notice, we will update the date at the top.
